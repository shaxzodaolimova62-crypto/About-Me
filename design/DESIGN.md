## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an editorial, serene, and sophisticated digital presence for an AI and digital creator. It blends high-end editorial publication aesthetics with organic tranquility—bridging technological innovation with human warmth, poise, and intellectual clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Refined & Cerebral:** Expresses high-tier taste, intentional precision, and avant-garde creative direction.
- **Organic & Grounded:** Leaves behind clinical digital tropes in favor of natural sunlight, gentle foliage, and tactility.
- **Calm Authority:** Evokes immediate trust, focus, and quiet confidence without aggressive visual noise.

### Design Movement: Organic Editorial Glassmorphism
The visual aesthetic pairs editorial print layouts with soft translucent glassmorphism. It uses generously rounded white/ivory card containers (`rounded-3xl`), ultra-fine translucent borders, soft ambient drop shadows tinted with deep sage, and botanical environmental backdrops to produce an elevated, magazine-like digital canvas.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in soft botanical greens, warm paper whites, and deep organic charcoals, creating a low-stress, high-elegance atmosphere.

### Color Roles & Meaning
- **Primary (`#737D5E` — Deep Olive):** Anchors primary call-to-actions, active navigation pills, highlighted key metrics, and key focus states.
- **Secondary (`#92977A` — Soft Olive Green):** Applied to secondary interactive surfaces, chip fills, hover highlights, and subtle accent lines.
- **Tertiary (`#E8E0D1` — Warm Beige):** Powers subtle background washes, decorative card accents, divider lines, and inner container fills.
- **Neutral (`#34372F` — Dark Charcoal):** Used for typography, high-contrast headings, and foundational vector iconography, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Background / Canvas (`#F8F6F0` — Warm Ivory):** The foundational substrate that provides warmth, mimicking uncoated fine cotton paper.
- **Surface Accent (`#DCE0D2` — Soft Sage):** Used for translucent card borders, subtle tag backgrounds, and floating utility pills.

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on a dual-personality structure:

1. **Editorial Sophistication (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for personal branding, display headings, testimonial pull-quotes, and high-impact project showcases. The high stroke contrast exudes the timeless craft of art catalogues and fashion publications.
2. **Modern Ergonomic Legibility (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides clarity for body copy, navigational pills, technical spec tags, and structural subheads.

### Typographic Rules
- **Category & Section Headers:** Always rendered in `section-title` styling—uppercase, bold, with generous tracking (`0.12em`) to ground card sections.
- **Editorial Subtitles:** Rendered in `Playfair Display` italic or regular weight when accompanying hero introductions.
- **Body Hierarchy:** Main narrative text uses `body-lg` with comfortable line height (`26px`) to ensure an unhurried, comfortable reading rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an organic fluid-within-max-width grid, emphasizing balanced card clusters and generous whitespace.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width canvas of 1200px centered against the botanical environment backdrop. Content structures in 2-column or 3-column asymmetric card arrangements with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 2-column stacked card layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** Single-column vertical stream with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` vertical flow gaps between modules.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Card padding remains generous: `1.5rem` to `2rem` on desktop, scaling to `1.25rem` on mobile.
- Section titles are separated from list content by `space-md` (1rem).
- Interactive card actions sit detached at the card bottom with `space-lg` separation from body text.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses atmospheric diffusion rather than sharp, drop-shadow realism.

### 1. Translucent Base Surfaces
Content panels utilize semi-translucent ivory backgrounds (`rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.88)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`) combined with a soft backdrop filter blur (`backdrop-blur-md`, 12px–16px). This allows the natural tones of the botanical background to faintly illuminate the cards without compromising readability.

### 2. Ambient Shadow Palette
Shadows are tinted with the natural olive tone rather than neutral gray:
- **Card Ambient:** `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(52, 55, 47, 0.08)`
- **Floating Bar / Modal:** `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(115, 125, 94, 0.16)`

### 3. Hairline Low-Contrast Borders
Cards and floating elements are enclosed with an ethereal outline:
- `1px solid rgba(220, 224, 210, 0.65)` on light card bodies.
- `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` along top and left edges to mimic soft overhead sunlit bevels.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a curvilinear visual language to echo organic and botanical motifs.

### Curvature Architecture
- **Primary Content Panels & Hero Cards:** Use ultra-rounded corners (`rounded-3xl` / 24px–32px), eliminating sharp angles.
- **Buttons, Badges & Navigation Links:** Adopt full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly tactile affordance.
- **Internal Media & Embedded Images:** Softened with `rounded-2xl` (16px) corners to nest naturally within parent card radiuses.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Enclosed in circular discs (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backgrounds.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), deep olive background (`#737D5E`), white text, subtle hover expansion, and gentle warm-shadow elevation (`rgba(115, 125, 94, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Soft Button:** Soft olive or sage background (`#92977A` or `#DCE0D2`), dark charcoal text, minimal transition to slightly deeper sage on hover.
- **Glass / Ghost Button:** Pill border with `1px solid rgba(115, 125, 94, 0.3)`, transparent background, deep charcoal text, filling to 10% olive tint upon focus.

### 2. Navigation Pill Bar
- Floating header container centered at the top of the canvas.
- Backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) with translucent ivory fill (`rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.85)`).
- Nav items styled as delicate pill links with subtle line icons and `label-md` typography.
- Active item encased in a warm beige/sage pill indicator (`#DCE0D2`).

### 3. Feature & Showcase Cards
- Translucent ivory cards with `rounded-3xl` corners, hairline sage borders, and soft ambient drop shadows.
- Inner layout structured with uppercase section headers (`section-title`), clean typography, and dedicated trailing action pills.
- Support for integrated image thumbnails featuring rounded inner borders and warm color-graded imagery.

### 4. Interactive List Items & Service Rows
- Paired with circular badge icons (36px–40px diameter) tinted in soft sage (`#DCE0D2` or `#E8E0D1`).
- Clean minimalist single-line stroke icons (1.5px stroke width, `#34372F`).
- Subtle trailing navigation chevrons (`>`) signaling interactive pathways.

### 5. Pill Badges & Chips
- Compact height (24px–28px) with `rounded-full` contours.
- Subtle background tint (`#E8E0D1` or `#DCE0D2`) with deep charcoal/olive text in `label-sm`.
- Used for AI discipline tags (e.g., "Generative Direction", "Prompt Craft", "Editorial Motion").

### 6. Social & Contact Connectors
- Symmetrical grid of contact points (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Email, LinkedIn).
- Circular touch targets (`rounded-full`) with soft neutral borders, transitioning to deep olive on hover with smooth spring transforms.
